## Week 1 - Excel Training
## **Executive Summary**

As part of my **Data Technician training**, I developed strong technical and analytical skills in managing, analysing, and presenting data. I gained a clear understanding of key data governance frameworks, including the **Data Protection Act (DPA)**, **General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)**, **Freedom of Information Act (FOI)**, and the **Computer Misuse Act**, ensuring that I handle data ethically, securely, and in compliance with legal standards.

Using **Microsoft Excel**, I created structured datasets, applied formulas such as `SUM` and `AVERAGE`, and built **Pivot Tables** and **SWITCH functions** to categorise and summarise data. These projects strengthened my ability to extract insights and transform raw data into meaningful information that supports informed decision-making.

I also enhanced my skills in **data visualisation and communication**, learning how to present analytical findings effectively to senior stakeholders through clear visuals and confident delivery.

This portfolio demonstrates my ability to combine **technical accuracy**, **data compliance**, and **effective communication** — transforming data into actionable insights that support business goals and data-driven decisions.
